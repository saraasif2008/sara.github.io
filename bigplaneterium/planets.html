10 THINGS ABOUT OUR SOLAR SYSTEM

1. The Sun holds almost everything

About 99.8% of the Solar System’s mass is in the Sun!

2. Earth isn’t perfectly round

Earth is slightly squished at the poles and bulges at the equator.

3. Venus is hotter than Mercury

Venus is hotter because of its thick atmosphere trapping heat.

4. Jupiter has a giant storm

The Great Red Spot is a storm bigger than Earth.

5. Saturn could float

Saturn has very low density.

6. Uranus spins sideways

Extreme tilt rotation.

7. Neptune fastest winds

Up to 2000 km/h.

8. Moon moving away

3.8 cm yearly.

9. Millions of asteroids

Asteroid belt region.

10. Solar System is huge

Voyager took 40+ years.

Solar System Facts

Our solar system includes the Sun, eight planets, five officially named dwarf planets, hundreds of moons, and thousands of asteroids and comets.

Our solar system is located in the Milky Way galaxy in the Orion Arm.

It takes about 230 million years to orbit the galaxy.
